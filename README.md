I am a Ph.D. researcher at HUST, undertaking joint research training with Tsinghua THUNLP and Beijing Zhongguancun Academy. My work focuses on embodied AI, vision-language-action models, robot learning, reasoning-oriented control, and the research systems that make these models easier to train and evaluate.

## Selected Research

### [DeepThinkVLA](https://github.com/OpenBMB/DeepThinkVLA)

`FIRST-AUTHOR RESEARCH`

DeepThinkVLA studies when explicit reasoning improves robot action. It reports a **97.0% average success rate on LIBERO** and a **79.0% zero-shot success rate on LIBERO-Plus**.

[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.15669) · [Code](https://github.com/OpenBMB/DeepThinkVLA) · [Models & data](https://huggingface.co/collections/yinchenghust/deepthinkvla)
